## Decision

The scorer ranks terra/medium first (lenient fit, then exact, then failures,
then latency): +0.009 lenient over luna/medium, with lower p50/p90 latency and
about half the completion tokens. The operator approved the switch on this
evidence.

## Limitations

- The lenient margin over luna/medium is **not statistically separable** (the
  95% intervals overlap); the case for terra is "not worse, faster, fewer
  tokens", not "clearly more accurate".
- terra/medium is **lower on exact fit** (82.8% vs 94.0%) and on new-profile
  family fit (30% vs 57%): it more often picks an acceptable rather than the
  preferred profile, or names a new profile outside the expected family. The
  reviewed plan still gates every assignment, so these show up as review
  edits, not wrong writes.
- The rubric is the operator's own review of one library (subjective choices
  are listed in `rubric-v1.json`); confidence is self-reported; latency depends
  on proxy load at run time.
- Re-run `make knowledge-benchmark` after a model or prompt change; the
  report's baseline is now terra/medium.
